Track 2: Research Publications (4 Papers)

April – August 2026

How This Works

After every Vizuara bootcamp, top students enter a 2-month research phase where they work on original research projects. You join as a research mentor — guiding 4-5 student teams on paper-worthy projects. The students do the heavy lifting (experiments, writing, implementation). Your role:

  • Industry perspective on problem framing and motivation
  • Feedback on experiment design and methodology
  • Review and refinement of paper drafts (using AI tools like Claude for efficiency)
  • Co-authorship on the final submissions

Time commitment: ~2-3 hours/week for 8 weeks. One 30-min check-in per team per week (can batch into a single 2-hour session). Paper review/feedback asynchronous via Google Docs + Claude.

The 4 Papers

Paper 1
Edge Inference Benchmarking
Systematic benchmarking of LLM inference across Mac Mini, Raspberry Pi 5, and Jetson Orin Nano — measuring tok/s, TTFT, memory, power, and quality across quantization levels.
Target: NeurIPS 2026 Workshop or EMNLP 2026
Paper 2
Speculative Decoding on Edge
Ablation study of speculative decoding on resource-constrained devices — draft model sizes, acceptance rates, and real-world speedups on Jetson and Apple Silicon.
Target: NeurIPS 2026 Workshop or MLSys 2027
Paper 3
Voice Pipeline Latency on Edge
End-to-end analysis of ASR + LLM + TTS pipeline latency on edge devices — where the bottlenecks are, what optimizations work, and what's needed for real-time voice AI.
Target: EMNLP 2026 or Interspeech 2026
Paper 4
Quantization Quality Frontier
Mapping the complete Pareto frontier of quality vs compression for edge deployment — perplexity, task accuracy, and generation quality across Q8 to Q2 on multiple model families.
Target: NeurIPS 2026 Main or COLM 2026
